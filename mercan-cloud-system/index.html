Employees & commission settings

Each employee's commission = the same period's net sales after bills × that employee's individual rate. Rates are applied separately, exactly as specified; combined rates can exceed 100%, so review your rates carefully.

Employee advances & salary deductions

Record cash advances and employee expenses, approve them, then deduct them from salary. Deductions are tracked here and are never counted as sales or income.

Employee financial summary

Remaining balance = approved expenses − deductions applied. Net salary before commission = monthly salary − deductions applied in the selected month; commissions and other earnings are added by the payroll module. Pending and rejected expenses are never deducted.
